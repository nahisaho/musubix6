## Design
Reuse the existing add/Finding/analyze pipeline and nine semantic checks. New dependency-based candidates apply only to prose mode and ignore quoted matches; AI editing guides preserve actors, conditions, uncertainty, tense, register and defined terms, and expose unresolved meaning rather than guess.
Reuse markdown-it-py, already used by invariant checks, for code-span masking; declare it in every affected PEP723 entry point. Reject ad-hoc backtick regexes and global bans on nonhuman subjects; do not impose yomiyasu sentence-length targets on existing genres.
